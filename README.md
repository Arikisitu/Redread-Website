# 🔴 RedRead

> **Read. Discover. Repeat.**

A modern manga reading experience built around simplicity, immersion, and discovery. 📖✨

RedRead combines a clean interface with a dark cinematic visual style to keep the focus where it belongs — on your reading experience. 🌙

---

## ✨ Why RedRead?

RedRead is designed with one idea in mind:

> **Less distraction. More reading.**

From browsing to reading, every part of the experience is designed to feel clear, smooth, and enjoyable. 🎯

---

## 🚀 Highlights

📖 **Focused Reading**  
Enjoy a clean interface designed to keep distractions to a minimum.

🔎 **Discover**  
Browse and explore manga through a simple, organized experience.

📚 **Personal Library**  
Keep the manga you care about close and organized.

🔐 **Source Management**  
Dedicated access for managing available manga sources.

🎨 **Custom Experience**  
A visual system built around RedRead's distinctive identity.

⚡ **Smooth Interface**  
Subtle animations and responsive interactions throughout the experience.

🌙 **Dark Cinematic Design**  
Deep blacks, rich burgundy tones, warm highlights, and atmospheric details.

## 🎨 Visual Identity

RedRead uses a carefully selected visual palette:

- `#0D0D0D` — Deep Black
- `#5A0F18` — Dark Burgundy
- `#F0443E` — Red Accent
- `#FFF1DC` — Warm Cream
- `#BFA99A` — Soft Neutral

The RedRead icon and visual language are carried consistently throughout the experience. 🔴

## 💭 Design Philosophy

RedRead aims to feel:

**Simple.**  
**Immersive.**  
**Focused.**  
**Distinctive.**
